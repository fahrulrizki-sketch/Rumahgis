## Prompt ilustrasi

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a polished 3D educational cutaway illustration for an Indonesian Instagram carousel, landscape 4:3, visual-dominant with almost no text. Show ocean at left and a green coastal landmass at right, with the oceanic tectonic plate descending gently from left to right underneath the overriding land plate. Clearly show the contact surface between the plates highlighted subtly in amber, two small opposing motion arrows along the plate interface. Calm sea, no tsunami wave, no destruction, no eruption, no fear imagery. Rock layers look like a museum geological model, earthy tones, turquoise ocean, natural daylight, off-white backdrop. Educational schematic, NOT a geographic map and NOT to scale. Exact Indonesian labels only: 'Lempeng samudra' on the subducting slab, 'Lempeng atas' on the overriding slab, 'Zona megathrust' with fine leader line to the shallow plate contact under the sea near the coast. Small readable footer 'Ilustrasi edukasi • Tidak berskala'. No title, no paragraphs, no other writing or logos. Make the process visually intelligible for non-experts.
